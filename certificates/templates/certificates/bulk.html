Adding the photos

  1. Click the cell in the PHOTO column for that student.
  2. Use Insert › Pictures › This Device and choose the photograph.
  3. Drag it so it sits on that student’s row — the row it sits on is what matches it to the student, so it need not be exactly inside the cell.
  • Every column is required, and every student needs a photo. SL_NO and REGISTRATION_NUMBER must each be unique — no two students may share either.
  • Use the ordinary Place over Cells insert. Excel’s newer Place in Cell pictures are stored differently and cannot be read back out — the app will tell you if it finds them.
  • Any photo size or shape works — there is no passport-size requirement. Whatever you insert is fitted to the certificate’s photo box ({{ photo_box_w }} × {{ photo_box_h }}) without being stretched.
  • If a photo is much smaller than the box it is enlarged and may print softer, and if it is a very different shape some of it is trimmed to fill the box. Either way the certificate is still made — you are just told what to expect. About {{ recommended_photo.0 }} × {{ recommended_photo.1 }} prints crisply if you have the choice.
  • A row with no photo is reported as an error, naming the cell to put it in.
  • The HOD signature is uploaded here, not in the spreadsheet — one signature is stamped on every certificate in the batch. Any size works; a white background is removed automatically, so a plain scan works.
  • Choose JPG or PDF above. PDF pages come out at the card’s real printed size (9 × 6 inches), so a printed certificate is actual size. You can also get every certificate in one PDF, a page each, ready to print.
  • Anything that would stop a certificate being made is listed here on upload, with the exact cell to edit.
  • Save the workbook as .xlsx. A .csv cannot carry photos, so it can no longer be used.
